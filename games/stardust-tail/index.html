How to Play

  • Drag anywhere, or use Arrow keys / WASD, to fly your comet.
  • Collect glowing stardust to add beads to your tail.
  • Collect three of the same shape in a row to earn a Prism bonus.
  • Avoid dark void shards. A hit costs a shield and scatters beads.
  • White rainbow motes restore a shield. Lose all three shields and the run ends.
  • Keyboard: Esc or P pauses. Press Esc to close any menu.

Quick Start

Fly with a drag or the Arrow keys. Collect stardust to grow your tail. Match three of the same shape for a Prism bonus, and avoid dark void shards.
