How to Play

  • Drag anywhere, or use Arrow keys / A-D, to steer your blob across the candy road.
  • Collect sprinkles for bonus points and a higher score.
  • Tap the Jump button, or press Space / Up, to hop over gummy cones and hurdles.
  • Do not fall into the chocolate gaps. Stay on the frosting.
  • You have three hearts. A hit or a fall costs one heart; lose all three and the run ends.
  • Keyboard: Esc or P pauses. Press Esc to close any menu.

Quick Start

Steer left and right to stay on the candy road. Tap or press Space to hop over gummy cones and the chocolate gaps. Collect sprinkles along the way.
